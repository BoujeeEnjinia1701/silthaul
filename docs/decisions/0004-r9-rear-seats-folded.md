# 0004: R9 accepted with the rear seats folded

- **Date:** 2026-10-03
- **Status:** decided by Amish, 2026-10-03 (round 2): "i agree with all the 46 recommendations you provided. please proceed." For SiltHaul this is decision 2A, the open decision in SLH-DEC-001 v0.2.

## Context

With the bolted flat-pack frame (SLH-DDR-003) the kit packs into about 454 L, 77 % of a small hatchback's load space with the rear seats folded (1,250 x 950 x 500 mm, 594 L). With the seats up, the 890 x 814 mm side frames do not lie flat in a typical 950 x 650 mm boot floor.

## Options considered

| Option | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| A (chosen) | Accept the rear seats folded as the reference; record R9 as met on paper |
| B | Split each side frame into bolted tubes so everything fits with the seats up: about forty bolts and well over 20 min of setup, which breaks R8 |

## Decision

Option A. R9 is restated as "fits a small hatchback with the rear seats folded; heaviest part 25 kg or less". No design change: the frame, the parts and the mass are as in SLH-DDR-003.

*Table 1. Effects.*

| Item | Result |
| --- | --- |
| R9 | Met on paper: heaviest lift 23.1 kg; kit about 454 L packed against 594 L |
| Other requirements | None changed; R8 stays about 20 min, at its limit |
| Cost | Unchanged. Value-engineering target: USD 2,000. Estimated cost of the constructable design: USD 1,055.60 (USD 944.40 under the target) |
| Documents | Requirements v0.5, calculation note v0.4 (wording only), build plan v0.3 (pack check wording), register v0.3 |

## Safety

> **Safety:** SiltHaul is a rope-and-anchor system under tension with a hand-cranked chain drive; proof-load tests come before use. Packing the heavy parts into a car is a manual-handling task: no lift is heavier than 25 kg, and two people share the drum with bearings (23.1 kg). Flood mud may carry sewage and disease; use boots, gloves and hygiene measures.
